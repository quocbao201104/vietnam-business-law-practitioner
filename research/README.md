# Research

Research remains first-class provenance rather than being discarded after synthesis.

For each BL track, research artifacts should preserve where useful:

- frozen research question;
- source map;
- findings and distinctions;
- contradictory evidence;
- rejected rules/folklore;
- architecture implications;
- unresolved questions;
- repair history.

Knowledge files are distilled practitioner artifacts. Research files explain why those artifacts exist.

Third-party packages, repositories, practitioner material, academic work, official sources, and legal-tech systems are research inputs. No single external package is the architectural source of truth.
