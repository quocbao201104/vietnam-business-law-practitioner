# BL4 — Breach / Remedies / Evidence / Disputes

## Owns

Breach/liability classification after BL3 state; excuse; remedy availability; notice; mitigation; loss/proof; evidence preservation; limitation/deadlines; settlement/dispute posture; invocation/procedure/remedies under a resolved or explicitly conditioned forum/regime.

## Does not own

What contract/obligation or dispute-resolution clause exists (BL3), cross-border governing-law/treaty/international-enforcement overlay (BL8), employment classification (BL6), regulatory legality (BL7).

## Activate when

There is non-performance, defective/late performance, threatened breach, notice/claim, loss, evidence urgency, settlement, or dispute escalation.

## Core distinctions

- deviation ≠ actionable breach;
- breach ≠ liability;
- liability ≠ every remedy available;
- force majeure ≠ generic bad event;
- hardship ≠ force majeure;
- penalty ≠ damages ≠ late-payment interest;
- having a remedy ≠ exercising it correctly;
- negotiation should not silently waive rights or deadlines;
- dispute-clause content ≠ cross-border overlay ≠ dispute posture/procedure.

## Decision procedure

1. Receive obligation/performance state and dispute-clause content from BL3 where material.
2. For cross-border matters, consume resolved/conditioned governing-law, treaty, and international-enforcement propositions from BL8 before resolving dependent forum/procedure/remedy propositions.
3. Classify breach/materiality and attribution.
4. Test contractual/statutory excuse and risk allocation.
5. Preserve position: notice, objection, evidence, mitigation, deadlines.
6. Identify business objective: preserve performance, recover money, exit, reduce loss, protect assets, or settle.
7. Map only legally available remedies.
8. Build loss/proof ledger where damages are material.
9. Choose dispute/settlement posture and urgent protection only within the resolved or explicitly conditioned forum/regime state.
10. If settlement changes obligations, signal/return the changed transaction state to BL3; BL4 does not silently rewrite BL3 contract state.

## Failure modes

Any delay treated as termination right; force-majeure keyword matching; penalty ceiling/base guessed from memory; alleged loss accepted without causation/proof; mitigation ignored; notice treated as paperwork; negotiation allowed to consume limitation; governing law/forum/arbitration/enforcement collapsed; BL4 independently resolving BL3 clause content or BL8 cross-border overlay.
