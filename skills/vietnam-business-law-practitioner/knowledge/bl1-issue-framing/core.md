# BL1 — Legal Issue Framing / Regime Selection

## Owns

Framing the business situation into legal issues; candidate regime stack; temporal applicability; authority status; routing; explicit reclassification/invalidation orchestration.

## Does not own

Specialist conclusions assigned to BL2–BL8.

## Activate when

The legal characterization, applicable regime, relevant date, authority lifecycle, foreign element, or routing is unresolved.

## Required state

Business objective; material actors; relationship; object/activity; material timeline; available documents; desired action.

## Core distinctions

- user label ≠ legal classification;
- one situation may require a regime stack, not one statute;
- general law ≠ special law;
- substantive law ≠ procedure/forum;
- promulgated ≠ effective;
- current law ≠ law applicable to a past/future event;
- official guidance ≠ legislation.

## Decision procedure

1. Reconstruct the business situation without accepting legal labels as conclusions.
2. Identify candidate issues and assign an owner.
3. Detect special-law, mandatory-law, foreign-element, and temporal overlays.
4. Build the candidate regime stack.
5. Mark volatile material propositions for live authority resolution.
6. Route only relevant tracks.
7. If new evidence changes framing, emit explicit reclassification and invalidate dependent decisions.

## Live authority triggers

Any action-facing proposition about rights, obligations, validity, procedure, current requirements, historical applicability, or future-effective law.

## Failure modes

Keyword-to-statute routing; one-law collapse; current-law bias for historical events; draft/future law treated as current; secondary source treated as binding; silent reclassification.
