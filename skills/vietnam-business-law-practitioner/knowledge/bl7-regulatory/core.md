# BL7 — Regulatory / Market Conduct / Business Compliance

## Owns

Regulatory perimeter; market-entry and ongoing conditions; public-law permission; consumer/advertising/e-commerce/competition/data/product triggers; compliance lifecycle; specialist regulatory activation.

## Does not own

Private contract formation (BL3), tax (BL5), employment classification (BL6), border/customs and foreign-investment overlays (BL8).

## Activate when

A business model, product, service, channel, customer relationship, market position, data flow, or conduct may be prohibited, conditioned, licensed, registered, notified, supervised, or otherwise regulated.

## Core distinctions

- business registration ≠ permission to operate;
- registered business line ≠ all regulatory conditions satisfied;
- absence from conditional-business list ≠ unregulated;
- entry compliance ≠ ongoing compliance;
- B2B contract logic ≠ B2C mandatory overlay;
- contract consent ≠ regulatory/privacy compliance;
- product may be marketable ≠ every advertising claim/channel permitted;
- contractually agreed ≠ competition-safe;
- compliance ≠ proof of compliance.

## Decision procedure

1. Map actor, activity, product/service, customer, channel, data, market position, and geography.
2. Detect prohibition, market-entry, license/approval, registration/notification, ongoing-condition, conduct, and event-triggered duties.
3. Identify specialist regime only when triggered by concrete facts.
4. Verify current regime before action when permission/compliance materially affects the decision.
5. Identify evidence needed to prove compliance.
6. Monitor model/change events that may change regulatory role.
7. Return blockers/conditions to the final decision and relevant BL track.

## Failure modes

Registration treated as operating permission; license treated as perpetual compliance; noun-based checklist dumping; contract consent treated as privacy compliance; small company treated as competition-exempt; customs clearance treated as domestic market permission; stale checklist used as current law.
