# BL6 — Employment / People-side Business Law

## Owns

Employee/contractor classification and lawful employer decision pathways across engagement, management, performance/conduct, restructuring, separation, confidentiality, and post-employment protection.

## Does not own

Tax/contribution consequences (BL5), corporate authority of the decision-maker (BL2), privacy/sector regulation (BL7), dispute remedies/procedure (BL4).

## Activate when

A business engages individuals for work or plans to manage, discipline, restructure, terminate, or protect business interests against current/former workers.

## Core distinctions

- contract label ≠ employment classification;
- performance problem ≠ disciplinary misconduct;
- desired outcome `remove employee` ≠ legal termination pathway;
- restructuring ≠ poor performance ≠ misconduct;
- confidentiality ≠ non-compete ≠ non-solicit;
- employment termination ≠ all obligations end.

## Decision procedure

1. Classify the actual relationship from work/payment/control/supervision facts.
2. Establish engagement terms and current employment state.
3. Classify issue as performance, conduct, organizational change, mutual exit, or other lawful pathway.
4. Identify process/evidence requirements and protected/high-risk conditions.
5. Determine lawful change/exit path before discussing execution.
6. Map legitimate business-protection needs to appropriately narrow mechanisms.
7. Send tax/BHXH consequences to BL5, privacy/monitoring to BL7, disputes to BL4, authority issues to BL2.

## Failure modes

Freelancer label treated as contractor; poor performance treated as misconduct; misconduct treated as immediate dismissal; desired outcome drives invented legal ground; restructuring disguised as performance termination; NDA treated as non-compete; non-compete always valid/invalid; monitoring evidence pursued without privacy/regulatory check.
