# BL8 — Investment / Cross-border / Trade

## Owns

Foreign-element classification; inbound/outbound investment overlay; market access; governing-law/treaty/CISG layer; FX/payment classification; goods-trade/customs interface; specialist trade/origin activation.

## Does not own

Corporate mechanics (BL2), contract obligations (BL3), tax consequences (BL5), domestic product/market permission (BL7), dispute remedies (BL4).

## Activate when

A party, owner/investor, performance location, asset, payment, governing law, or goods movement has a foreign element that can change the legal regime.

## Core distinctions

- cross-border ≠ import/export;
- corporate validity ≠ foreign-investment compliance;
- ownership percentage ≠ complete market-access analysis;
- governing law ≠ forum ≠ arbitral procedure ≠ enforcement jurisdiction;
- international sale ≠ automatic application of Vietnamese commercial law;
- foreign invoice payment ≠ foreign loan ≠ capital contribution;
- product name ≠ HS classification;
- classification ≠ origin ≠ preferential tariff entitlement;
- FTA exists ≠ zero tariff;
- Incoterm ≠ complete legal allocation;
- contractual import-cost allocation ≠ statutory customs responsibility.

## Decision procedure

1. Map the foreign element and classify cross-border mode: investment, contract/service, payment/loan, or goods trade.
2. For investment, resolve market-access/foreign-investor overlay and return corporate mechanics to BL2.
3. For contracts, resolve governing law/treaty/CISG issues before BL3 where material.
4. For payments, classify current/capital/loan flow and coordinate tax with BL5.
5. For goods, activate trade-policy → HS → origin → valuation/tariff → customs chain only as needed; use specialist depth for technical classification/origin.
6. Send domestic product/market permission to BL7.
7. Resolve law for the planned transaction/border/payment date, including future-effective changes when material.

## Failure modes

Foreign party treated as import/export; Vietnam party treated as automatic Vietnamese governing law; FTA-country shipment treated as preferential origin; HS guessed from name; DDP treated as statutory responsibility transfer; foreign ownership percentage used as sole access test; future law treated as current.
