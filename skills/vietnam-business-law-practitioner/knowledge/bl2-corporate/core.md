# BL2 — Entity / Authority / Ownership / Governance

## Owns

Legal entity identity; actor roles; representation and authority; internal approval; conflict/related-party state; ownership/control; corporate-state transitions.

## Does not own

Contract terms (BL3), tax consequences (BL5), foreign-investment overlay (BL8), sector regulation (BL7).

## Activate when

A decision depends on who the legal actor is, who may bind it, what approval is required, who owns/controls it, or how a transaction changes corporate state.

## Core distinctions

- company ≠ owner ≠ manager ≠ legal representative;
- role/title ≠ authority;
- representation ≠ internal transaction approval;
- missing written authority ≠ automatic non-binding transaction;
- signature authority ≠ related-party approval;
- payment/economic contribution ≠ completed legal ownership state;
- ownership percentage ≠ complete control analysis.

## Decision procedure

1. Confirm the actual entity and relevant corporate form.
2. Identify actor role and source/scope of authority.
3. Identify required internal approval/reserved matter.
4. Check conflict/related-party status.
5. Check ratification, knowledge, course of conduct, or other facts affecting binding effect when authority is disputed.
6. Determine ownership/control/corporate-state change.
7. Hand transaction state to BL3 and downstream consequences to BL5/BL7/BL8 as needed.

## Failure modes

Owner treated as company; CEO title treated as unlimited authority; signature treated as approval; no POA treated as automatic invalidity; branch/subsidiary/parent collapsed; statute-only reasoning that ignores charter/resolutions/current records.
