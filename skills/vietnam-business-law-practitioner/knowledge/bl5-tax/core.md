# BL5 — Tax / Financial Legal Consequences

## Owns

Tax characterization of a resolved business transaction; taxpayer/withholder roles; taxable-event mapping; documentation/timing; tax-aware economic consequences; current-rule verification triggers.

## Does not own

Bookkeeping/accounting entries; employment classification (BL6); corporate validity (BL2); contract formation (BL3); customs specialist classification (BL8).

## Activate when

A decision may affect VAT, corporate/personal income tax, withholding/foreign-contractor treatment, invoice/document conditions, deductibility, contributions, tax incentives, or transaction economics.

## Core distinctions

- business payment label ≠ tax characterization;
- one transaction may create multiple tax positions;
- contractual tax allocation ≠ statutory tax liability;
- invoice validity ≠ VAT credit ≠ tax deductibility ≠ accounting recognition;
- cash movement ≠ tax recognition;
- incentive category match ≠ incentive entitlement;
- tax optimization should be reframed as tax-aware structuring.

## Decision procedure

1. Receive resolved transaction/employment/corporate/cross-border characterization from owning tracks.
2. Identify taxpayer, payer, recipient, withholder, and taxable events.
3. Identify candidate tax regimes and current-law propositions requiring live verification.
4. Resolve current base/method/rate/threshold/timing only from appropriate current authority.
5. Test documentary/payment/evidence conditions.
6. Separate substantive, documentary, procedural, and interpretive risk.
7. Return cash/economic/structuring consequences to BL2/BL3/BL6/BL8 where material.

## Failure modes

Rates/thresholds from memory; invoice treated as automatic deductibility; cash paid/received treated as tax trigger universally; foreign supplier mapped to one fixed treatment; freelancer label drives PIT; tax clause rewrites statutory duty; draft/guidance treated as binding law; lowest-tax option treated as automatically best structure.
