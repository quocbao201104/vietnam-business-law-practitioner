# BL3 — Contracts / Commercial Transactions

## Owns

Formation; contract/document stack; contractual terms; obligations; conditions; performance state; evidence path; variation and course of dealing.

## Does not own

Signatory authority (BL2), breach/remedies (BL4), statutory tax liability (BL5), regulatory permission (BL7), cross-border governing-law/treaty resolution (BL8).

## Activate when

The decision requires establishing what deal exists, what documents govern, what each party must do, whether conditions/performance occurred, or whether the deal changed.

## Core distinctions

- contract ≠ signed PDF;
- negotiation ≠ agreement;
- formation ≠ validity ≠ effectiveness ≠ performance;
- written text ≠ complete contract position;
- negotiated term ≠ standard term;
- obligation should have a proof path;
- one invalid term ≠ automatic invalidity of the entire relationship.

## Decision procedure

1. Receive governing-regime/authority inputs from BL1/BL8 and authority inputs from BL2 where material.
2. Establish formation and transaction characterization.
3. Reconstruct the document stack and precedence/chronology.
4. Map obligations as who/what/standard/when/condition/proof.
5. Establish actual performance and disputed performance.
6. Identify valid changes, waivers, later agreements, and course of dealing.
7. Send deviations/non-performance to BL4; send tax clauses/economics to BL5; send mandatory-law concerns to BL7.

## Failure modes

Signed-PDF bias; draft treated as contract; missing seal treated as automatic invalidity; every email treated as amendment; written contract treated as entire relationship; digital agreement treated as always requiring digital signature; special-law/cross-border overlay ignored.
